# Pink Doll Mansion Privacy Policy

**Website:** https://pinkdollmansion.com
**Privacy policy URL:** https://pinkdollmansion.com/privacy
**Contact:** hello@pinkdollmansion.com
**Last updated:** 10 November 2026

Pink Doll Mansion (pinkdollmansion.com) operates this website and its account, membership and private-content features. This policy explains what information may be processed when you visit the website, create an account, sign in with Google or email, purchase a membership or item, or communicate through the website.

## Who operates Pink Doll Mansion

Pink Doll Mansion (pinkdollmansion.com) operates this website and its account, membership and private-content features. For privacy questions or requests, contact hello@pinkdollmansion.com. This policy applies to information processed when you visit the website, create an account, sign in with Google or email, purchase a membership or item, or communicate through the website.

## Information we collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may process your email address; name and username; account and authentication identifiers; profile details you choose to provide; account status; membership plan, dates and access level; content purchases; payment and invoice references; messages and attachments you send; and preferences such as whether you have chosen to receive marketing updates. We do not ask you for your Google password, Gmail messages, contacts, Google Drive files or calendar data.

## Information received when you use Google Sign-In

If you choose Continue with Google, Google provides basic account information needed to identify you, such as your Google account email address, name and, if available, profile image and provider account identifier. The sign-in flow is intended to use basic identity/sign-in information only. We do not request permission to read your Gmail inbox, send Gmail messages, access your Google Contacts, Google Drive or Google Calendar. You authenticate with Google; Pink Doll Mansion does not receive or store your Google password.

## How we use Google account information

We use the basic Google profile and account information only to authenticate you, create or match your Pink Doll Mansion account, populate the name and email fields of your account where available, maintain your signed-in session, protect the account and provide account-related support. We may use your email address and name to address a transactional welcome or account email that is sent through our email provider. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it for purposes unrelated to providing and securing your account.

## How Google account information is stored and shared

Google sign-in is handled through Supabase Authentication. The identity information returned by Google is processed by Supabase so that Supabase can authenticate the account and maintain the user record. The website stores the account identifier and the profile information needed to operate your account in its Supabase database. Where an email or welcome message is required, the relevant email address and limited personalisation details may be passed to Resend for delivery. We do not sell or rent Google user data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. We do not send Google profile data to the Bitcoin payment provider as part of sign-in.

## Account, profile and security information

When you register by email or Google, the website maintains an account record and a profile. Profile information may include your name, username, avatar/profile image, account role and account status. Authentication providers also process information needed to verify sign-in and protect accounts. You are responsible for keeping your sign-in method and devices secure. Do not share your password, one-time codes or account recovery links.

## Memberships, purchases and Bitcoin payments

If you buy a membership or individual item, the website records the selected plan or item, price and currency, order status, invoice reference, payment-provider reference, payment timestamps and, when supplied by the provider, Bitcoin amount, exchange-rate information and transaction identifier. Bitcoin invoices and payment confirmation are handled through BTCPay Server. The website does not request or store your wallet seed phrase or private keys. Bitcoin transactions may be recorded on a public blockchain and may be publicly visible outside our control. We use payment information to create invoices, reconcile settlement, activate or expire access, prevent duplicate fulfilment, handle support requests and maintain financial and security records.

## Emails and notifications

We use transactional email services to send messages such as email confirmation, password reset, sign-in/account notices, welcome messages, membership or purchase confirmations, and service notifications. Resend is used to deliver website emails from the configured Pink Doll Mansion sender address. The email address and the minimum information needed to prepare and deliver a message are shared with the email service. Optional promotional updates and membership reminders are sent only when you have opted in; you can withdraw that choice through your account preferences or an unsubscribe link where provided. Essential service and security emails may still be sent when needed to operate your account.

## Private messages and uploaded attachments

If you use the private conversation feature, we process the text of messages and any photographs, voice notes or other attachments you choose to send, together with related delivery and read-status information. These materials are stored in restricted website storage and are intended to be visible only to the conversation participants and authorised website operator. Do not send information or images that you do not have the right to share. Private links may be time-limited; however, no online service can guarantee that a recipient will not copy, photograph or otherwise record content they are authorised to view.

## Website content and access information

The website stores content descriptions, categories, publication status, access level and purchase or membership permissions so it can show public previews and check whether an account may open protected content. Private original photographs and videos are intended to be kept in restricted storage and delivered only after an access check. Public preview images may be displayed on public pages. We do not intentionally publish private original media as public gallery content.

## Cookies, browser storage and technical logs

The website uses essential browser storage and authentication session information to keep you signed in, complete OAuth redirects, remember necessary preferences and protect the service. Cloudflare, which provides website delivery and security services, and Supabase, which provides backend and authentication services, may process technical information such as IP address, browser/device details, request timestamps and security or diagnostic logs as part of operating and protecting their services. You can clear browser storage through your browser settings, but doing so may sign you out or affect site functionality. We do not describe optional analytics or advertising cookies here; if such tools are added, this policy and any required consent choices will be updated before they are used.

## Service providers and other disclosures

We use service providers to operate the website: Supabase for authentication, database, storage and backend functions; Google as the sign-in identity provider when you choose Google Sign-In; Resend for transactional email delivery; Cloudflare for hosting, content delivery, DNS and security; and BTCPay Server for Bitcoin invoices and payment-status processing. These providers process information as needed to provide their services and under their own terms and privacy notices. We may also disclose information if reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to valid legal requests, protect users or the website, investigate suspected fraud or enforce our terms. We do not sell personal information.

## How long we keep information

We keep account and profile information while your account is active and for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes, enforce agreements and meet applicable record-keeping obligations. Membership, purchase, invoice and payment records may need to be retained after an account is closed for accounting, fraud-prevention or legal reasons. Messages and attachments may remain available while needed to operate the conversation or resolve a report. Retention periods can differ by data type and by the backup and deletion schedules of our service providers.

## Your privacy choices and requests

You may ask to access, correct or request deletion of personal information associated with your account, subject to identity checks and information we are legally or operationally required to retain. You may also change profile details and your optional marketing preference in account settings. To make a privacy request, email hello@pinkdollmansion.com from the address associated with your account and use the subject line Privacy Request. Explain whether you are requesting access, correction, deletion or another action. We will review the request and respond as required by applicable law. Deleting an account does not remove information already recorded on a public blockchain or records that must lawfully be retained.

## Marketing preferences

Promotional emails and membership reminders are optional. We send them only when you have opted in. You can turn off the marketing preference in account settings or use the unsubscribe link in a promotional email. Unsubscribing from promotional messages does not prevent essential account, payment, security or service emails.

## Security

We use measures designed to protect account and website information, including authenticated access, database access policies, restricted storage for private originals, server-side checks for protected content and secure, time-limited media links. No website, transmission method or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Please use a strong unique password where applicable and contact us promptly if you believe your account has been accessed without permission.

## International processing

Our service providers may process or store information in countries other than the country where you live. Where information is transferred internationally, the relevant provider's infrastructure and contractual safeguards may apply. By using the website, you understand that service delivery may involve such processing, subject to applicable privacy law.

## Adults only

Pink Doll Mansion is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly invite or permit people under 18 to create accounts or use member features. If you believe a person under 18 has provided personal information, contact hello@pinkdollmansion.com so we can review the report and take appropriate action.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website, its service providers or its data practices change. The current version will be published at https://pinkdollmansion.com/privacy with the date it was last updated. Material changes will be highlighted on the website or communicated where required.
