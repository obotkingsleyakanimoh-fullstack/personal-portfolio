OWNER STUDIO
- Open /admin and select Appearance.
- Replace the seven fixed artwork positions: homepage hero, homepage introduction, homepage membership feature, About portrait, checkout portrait, Videos feature, and login/register artwork.
- Upload still images in JPEG, PNG, WebP or AVIF; they are resized and converted to WebP.
- Upload short public-safe feature videos in MP4 or WebM. They remain video files, not WebP.
- Alternatively, select a published public-safe WebP cover from My Content to reuse it in a fixed artwork position.
- Adjust the nine global website colours and save the palette.
- In Content, use the up/down arrows to arrange the gallery and video library order. This also controls the homepage's three featured collection cards.

STORAGE / PRIVACY
- `site-assets` is public and is only for non-explicit artwork and public-safe short video teasers.
- `content-previews` is public and stores non-explicit WebP gallery/video covers.
- `private-content` remains private and stores full original photographs and videos.
- `private-messages` remains private for private chat attachments.
- Unsplash URLs remain as fallbacks until the owner replaces a slot.
